The control plane

Serious delivery, without the ESP lock-in

Everything between your code and the recipient's inbox — built to be auditable and reliable, not a black box.

01

Bounded Retries

Configurable attempts with exponential backoff. Retries are scheduled, never blocking your pipeline.

02

Auto-Failover

Flips to a standby SMTP provider when the active one starts failing repeatedly.

03

Suppression Guards

Bounce, complaint, and unsubscribe are enforced on the way out — your reputation stays clean.

04

Domain Readiness

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checks before you send. Catch config issues early.

How it works

From API call to inbox, in four steps

One place to control and supervise every send — instead of scattered scripts and provider dashboards.

Step 1

Enqueues

Your app posts a message. It's validated, rate-limited, and queued.

Step 2

Checks

Suppression, unsubscribe, and blocked-domain rules are applied before dispatch.

Step 3

Relays

A worker sends via your SMTP, retrying with backoff and failing over when needed.

Step 4

Observes

Timeline, logs, incident bundles, and webhooks tell you exactly what happened.
